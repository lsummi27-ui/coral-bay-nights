Coral Bay Nights is an original top-down open-world crime game in the sun-soaked city of Coral Bay. Rook is back with $60, a borrowed jacket and a debt to the Marlin Crew.

## What is in the game

- A city to explore on foot and by car: roads, buildings, parks, a beach and docks
- Traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive
- A wanted level with police cars that chase and ram
- Four missions and a story: First Wheels, The Drop, Payback, The Getaway
- Combat on foot: punch and pistol, rival gang members who shoot back
- A HUD with health, money, wanted stars, ammo and a live minimap
- A start screen, pause, game over, and a save that resumes your progress
- Synthesised sound: engine, sirens, gunshots and mission jingles (no audio files)

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. WASD moves, Shift runs, E steals or enters a car, F punches, Space shoots, P pauses, M mutes.
